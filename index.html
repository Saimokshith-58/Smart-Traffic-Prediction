THE PROBLEM

Students shouldn't have to guess when their bus will arrive.

Today, many college bus systems depend on drivers manually sharing their location. Students check messages, call friends, or simply wait at the stop.

CampusRide changes the flow: GPS sends the bus position automatically, while an ETA engine uses route progress and traffic conditions to estimate arrival.
